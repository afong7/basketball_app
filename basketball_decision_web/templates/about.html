How do I use this app?

  1. Coaches (or other users) upload some game film. It could be separate video clips or whole quarters/halves/games.
  2. Choose your pause time (right before an action happens) and reveal time (when the correct answer is shown).
  3. After the clip pauses (at the pause time), players choose from a multiple choice question what they think is the best decision.
  4. The video plays until the reveal time, where an explanation will pop up explaining what the best decision is and why. (The explanation can be inputted or verbally stated)

The Mission

How times has your coach urged you to think or not to just go through the motions or to read the defense? I've told the players I've coached that basketball is a physical game, yes, but it's also a mental game. You have to outthink your opponent on offense and defense. However, a lot of players don't know how to visualize this.
Whether you're a beginner trying to understand the mental side of basketball or you're a serious player who loves to watch film, by watching video clips from your own games or from professionals (NBA, WNBA, etc.), the hope is you will develop/enhance your basketball IQ through real game situations and learn what it means to think the game.

For Coaches

For coaches, you can either upload your own game film, or just use the default videos. However, if you upload your own film, you must choose pause times and reveal times (i.e. you must go through the film yourself).
